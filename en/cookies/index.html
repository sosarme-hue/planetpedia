Cookie policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Planetpedia does not set any first-party or third-party cookies while you browse, and uses no analytics or advertising. That is why we do not show you a cookie banner.

YouTube videos

Videos do not load until you click them. When you click one, it plays from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. During playback, YouTube may store cookies or similar data in your browser to make the video work and measure its use. See Google's cookie policy. If you prefer to avoid this, do not play the videos.

How to delete or block cookies

You can view, delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge…), usually under privacy.

Changes

If we ever add non-essential cookies, we will ask you first and update this page.
