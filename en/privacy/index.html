What data is processed

  • Email. If you write to us, we will use your address and message only to reply. We do not share them with anyone. The legal basis is your consent in writing to us.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Hostinger), like any web server, logs IP addresses and pages visited, for security and for a limited time.
  • Fonts. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Videos. If you click a video, it loads from YouTube (Google) in privacy-enhanced mode. From then on, Google's privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to partenopides@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
